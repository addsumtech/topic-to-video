# HardBarrier

**Identity:** an approach that steps over the conditions effort can close, and stops dead at a masonry face with no opening anywhere in it. The wall's courses ARE the named barriers, so its height is the count and nothing is invented to fill it.

**Use when:** the beat's claim is that several named requirements are NOT the same kind of thing — some are a step you close by learning it, rewording it or doing it, and at least one is a wall where no amount of effort or phrasing opens anything, and the only honest move is to say so. **Not when:** every condition is closeable at some cost — that is a distance, and `LastLeg` argues it. Not when the claim is a ceiling you can approach but not pass (`SpeedLimit`). Not when the rows really are peers: then a list register is right and this one is an overstatement.

**Why it is not a list.** A list says every row is the same KIND of thing, which is exactly what this claim denies. Drawn as four identical bullets, "no reframing closes a legal barrier" is a sentence sitting beside four rows that contradict it; the frame argues the opposite of its own narration and passes every gate doing so. Drawn here, the approach is visibly stopped by the face of the wall, so the picture carries the argument with the caption hidden — which is the test this register exists to pass.

**Motion core:** the approach advances on `cadence`, stepping over each gap in turn, and arrives at the wall before the wall's own courses have finished setting — so the stop reads as a collision rather than as a diagram assembling. Nothing moves after the face is complete: the settled frame carries the whole claim, which is what every still gate and every reviewer reads.

| Prop | What it sets | Feel of turning it |
|---|---|---|
| `conditions` | the named conditions, 1 to `MAX_BARRIER_CONDITIONS` (8), `{label, kind}` | `kind` is `'gap'` or `'barrier'` and is the FILM's reading — the register infers it from nothing, least of all from the row's position |
| `subject` | what is being approached | in the film's words |
| `gapLabel` | what the stepped-over conditions are called | e.g. 「改得动」 |
| `wallLabel` | what the face is called | e.g. 「没有门的那一条」 |
| `kicker` | the spaced label over the run | uppercase follows the DNA's `uppercaseLabels` |
| `note` | one line under the frame, in the film's voice | where to say what the wall MEANS — the frame shows it and does not name it |

**Known traps:**
- **A board where NOTHING is a `barrier` is a refusal, not a warning.** That frame draws a run with a clear road at the end of it — the opposite claim, convincingly, past every gate. If no condition is a wall, the beat is a distance and this is the wrong register.
- A condition with no `label` throws, and more than 8 throws: past that the courses are too thin to read as masonry and the form stops arguing.
- The defaults are generic placeholder copy on purpose, so a film that forgets to pass its own is caught by the demo-copy gate rather than shipping the sample words.
- Its fixture needs the union member its refusal names; `cjk_type_qa._refused_member` supplies exactly one `'barrier'` row, so both branches render. Before that rule existed this register could not be fed at all and its `prop_reach` row read "could not be measured".
