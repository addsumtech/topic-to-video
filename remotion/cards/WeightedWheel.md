# WeightedWheel

**Identity:** the pick as a loaded roll: a wheel whose slices are the tokens' chances, spun again and again with each real landing written out; and the same slices reshaped by the temperature setting without a new name

**Use when:** unequal odds with one outcome per roll: a loaded wheel spun again and again with each landing written out, or the same named slices reshaped by a setting such as temperature.

**Motion core:** the wheel spins and lands on a slice per roll; in temperature mode the slices swell and shrink as the dial walks its scale and back
