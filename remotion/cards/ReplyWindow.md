# ReplyWindow

**Identity:** the chat window and what is (and is not) behind it: a reply streaming in; the two wrong pictures (a drawer of stored answers, a finished draft under a cover) struck; search results as more text to read; streaming against waiting; the cover lifted onto a blank page while the next piece is rolled at the caret; and a reader checking the fact

**Use when:** a beat about what a chat app shows: a reply streaming in under a caret, two windows compared (streaming vs waiting, a stored answer vs a finished draft, chances computed vs web results handed in as text), or one fact in a finished reply checked.

**Motion core:** the reply streams word by word and scrolls under a soft fade; with look 'float' the window is a lit slab that leans back, sways slowly and carries a moving sheen, its words level on the face
